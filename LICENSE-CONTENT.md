# Licences for documentation and course content

The **code** in this repository is licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).

Two other kinds of material use Creative Commons licences, which are written for text and pictures rather than
software:

| What | Where | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| The course format specification and the authoring guide | `docs/` | [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| The sample courses: lessons, pictures, sounds and their signed `.course` files | `packages/player-web/public/courses/`, `packages/player-web/public/bundles/` | [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) |

CC BY 4.0 lets anyone build their own player or tools for the format, as long as they credit Tome.
CC BY-SA 4.0 lets anyone reuse and adapt the sample courses, as long as they credit Tome and share their
adaptations under the same licence.

Pictures that a course credits to someone else keep the licence named in that course's credits.

Courses you write yourself with the Tome tools are yours, under whatever licence you choose.
